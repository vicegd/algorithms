## 🤝 Code of Conduct

### Our Commitment
We are committed to providing a **welcoming, safe, and inclusive environment** for all contributors, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

### Expected Behavior
* ✅ **Be Respectful:** Treat fellow contributors and students with professional courtesy.
* ✅ **Welcome Perspectives:** Encourage constructive discussions and diverse technical approaches.
* ✅ **Focus on Learning:** Remember that this repository is primarily an educational tool for students.
* ✅ **Graceful Feedback:** Give and accept constructive feedback with empathy.

### Unacceptable Behavior
* ❌ Harassment, derogatory comments, or personal insults.
* ❌ Plagiarism, copyright violations, or submitting code without proper attribution.
* ❌ Swallowing exceptions or publishing intentionally obfuscated code.

### Reporting Issues
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please notify the project maintainers directly via email.

Found a bug, an integer overflow vulnerability, or an edge-case failure? Please open an Issue.
